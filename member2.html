About me

Biography and skills

My name is Hassan Alali, and I study Information Technology – Multimedia at the University of Sharjah. I chose this major because it brings together technology, creativity, and digital design. I enjoy finding ways to use technology to create something useful and interesting.

University life. I’m learning graphic design, programming, and interactive multimedia. My coursework helps me become more creative, manage my time, and take responsibility for my work.

Hobbies and interests. Cycling is a big part of my life. Every ride challenges me to get stronger and improve my performance. It has taught me discipline, patience, and determination. I also enjoy going to the gym and staying active.

Future goals. I want to use what I learn to create meaningful digital projects and build a career in multimedia. I hope to keep improving as a cyclist, represent the UAE in sport one day, and make my family proud while giving back to my country.
