About me

Design meets purpose.

Hello, my name is Abdalla Adnan Almansoori. I am a UOS student who is currently majoring in the Bachelor of Multimedia in the College of Computing.

Originally, I was an engineering student back in 2024 but eventually transferred to Multimedia due to a lack of interest and a little background experience with graphic design and multimedia, 3D modelling to be exact.

My Hobbies and Interests

I work on 3D modelling and, more recently, 3D printing as a personal hobby.

I have printed things like decorations, miniature figures, firearm prop replicas, and helmets.

It is a very time-consuming hobby. However, it is very rewarding, and seeing the results after hours of work is satisfying.

On top of 3D modelling and printing, I am also learning model painting and designing, which are useful skills for improving the products I create through my main hobby.
